Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit and its website handle information when you use the service.

1. Information Orbit Uses

Orbit may process Discord information needed to provide its features, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message content where a feature requires it, moderation records, ticket data and configuration settings.

2. Licence Information

Orbit stores licence information needed to activate and enforce a licence, including the licensed server and licence status. Licence keys are stored in a protected form rather than as plain-text keys where applicable.

3. Website

The Orbit website does not require an account to browse. Purchase links send you to Stripe for payment processing. Stripe may collect and process payment and customer information under its own privacy policy.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate Orbit, provide requested features, maintain server configuration, enforce licensing, provide support and improve reliability and security.

5. Data Sharing

Information is not sold as a product. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the website, payments or Orbit, including Discord and Stripe.

6. Data Retention

Configuration and feature data may be retained for as long as needed to operate Orbit or maintain licence and support records. Retention can vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

7. Your Choices

If you have a privacy request or need information about data associated with your use of Orbit, contact Orbit Support through the support server.

8. Children's Privacy

Orbit is intended for users who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's own age requirements and applicable law.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when Orbit's features or data practices change.
